Good to Know

Planning Information

Lead Times

Most orders require at least 7 days' notice. Custom cakes require at least 10 days. Rush orders are subject to availability and a 10% surcharge.

Deposits

Orders totaling $200 or more require a 50% deposit to reserve your date.

Pickup and Delivery

Pickup is available. Delivery to public locations within 10 miles is $3, with additional mileage billed at $0.67 per mile.

Please Note

Menu items, pricing, and availability may change. Final pricing may vary based on design, size, flavor, customization, and availability.
