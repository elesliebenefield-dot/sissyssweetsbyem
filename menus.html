Good to Know

Ordering Details

Lead Time

Most items need at least 7 days' notice. Custom cakes need at least 10 days. Rush orders (less lead time) are subject to a 10% surcharge and depend on availability — always ask.

Deposits

Orders totaling $200 or more require a 50% deposit upfront to hold your date.

Delivery

Delivery is available to public locations within 10 miles for $3. Additional miles are charged at $0.15/mile. Pickup is also available.

Payment Methods

Cash · Check · Zelle (@sissyssweetsbyem) · Venmo (@sissyssweetsbyem) · Cash App ($sissyssweetsbyem) · Credit/Debit cards via QuickBooks

How to Order

Text or email Emily with the item you want, your preferred date, and any design details. She'll confirm availability and discuss the details with you directly.
