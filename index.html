Meet the Baker

Hi, I'm Emily — and baking is my love language.

I grew up in a culinary household with my dad as a chef, so the kitchen has always felt like home. What started as a passion for baking became a real business after friends and family kept telling me I had something special.

I completed the UGA Small Business Development Center course, earned my food handler's license, and officially launched Sissy's Sweets by Em — because I believe every celebration deserves something made with real care and creativity.

Based in Young Harris, Georgia.

— Emily Vargas
