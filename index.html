Meet Emily

The Baker Behind Sissy's Sweets

I grew up in a culinary household with my dad as a chef, so the kitchen has always felt like home. Baking was always my thing — and when friends and family kept telling me I had something special, I decided to make it official.

I completed the UGA Small Business Development Center course, earned my food handler's license, and launched Sissy's Sweets by Em.

Based in Young Harris, Georgia.

— Emily Vargas
